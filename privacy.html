Privacy

Your data stays under your control.

WebDevGym uses only the data required for sign-in, learning progress synchronization, and the features you choose to use.

Data we process

  • when you sign in with Google or GitHub: your account identifier, name, email address, and avatar when supplied by the provider;
  • learning progress, preferences, and profile data that you save in WebDevGym;
  • data created by local tools may remain only in your browser on your device.

How data is used

We use this data to authenticate you, restore your session, synchronize progress between devices, and display your profile. WebDevGym does not sell personal data or use it for advertising profiles.

Storage and service providers

Authentication and cloud storage are provided by Supabase. Google and GitHub sign-in are governed by their respective policies. The website is hosted on Vercel. Each service processes data only for its assigned role.

Data deletion

You can sign out and keep using WebDevGym locally. To request deletion of your cloud account and associated data, open a request in the WebDevGym repository.

Policy updates

This page will be updated when our data processing practices change. Last updated: October 28, 2026.
